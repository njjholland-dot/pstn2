The Problem with Current PSTN

Today's all-IP PSTN has critical vulnerabilities — and it has still not become the de facto person-to-person signalling solution for all-IP communications.

🚨 Rampant Fraud

Caller ID can be trivially spoofed, enabling scammers to impersonate banks, government agencies, and trusted organizations. $38 billion lost annually to phone fraud.

📍 Legacy Emergency Services

Emergency service systems are based on legacy technologies, with inaccurate or missing location data, costing precious minutes in life-threatening situations and leading to preventable deaths.

💰 High Costs

Multiple intermediaries to reach a destination create unnecessary complexity and expense.

🔒 No Real Authentication

The current system has no reliable way to verify where a call originated, creating challenges for law enforcement.

📋 Lack of standards

No standard way to communicate between industry participants makes automation challenging and adds to industry costs.

🛡️ Poor future protection

The industry invests little in its future; buying from, rather than selling to, over-the-top technology plays.

The PSTN2 Solution

A revolutionary architecture built on distributed messaging, cryptographic authentication, and direct communication

🔐

Cryptographic Authentication

Every call is cryptographically signed by the originating carrier, making spoofing mathematically impossible. Trust is built into the protocol.

🌐

Distributed Architecture

No single point of failure. The network uses eventual consistency across distributed databases, ensuring high availability and global scalability.

⚡

Direct Routing

Carriers connect directly to each other for reduced costs, lower latency, multi-media calls, and ability to exchange any other data package.

🚑

Enhanced Emergency Services

Accurate location data sent directly to emergency services with every 999 call, saving lives through faster, more precise response.

📱

Small CP Support

Low barrier to entry via MAPs (Managed Access Providers) who, like today's wholesalers, deal with the systems that smaller providers do not have the expertise to deal with.

🔄

Backward Compatible

Seamless interoperability with existing PSTN. Incremental deployment allows carriers to adopt PSTN2 features and functions at their own pace.

PSTN2 Architecture Overview

How Communication Providers connect to both legacy PSTN and modern PSTN2 networks

🔐

Authentication

Ed25519 digital signatures cryptographically verify every call's origin, making caller ID spoofing mathematically impossible.

🎯

Identification

Verified caller ID with rich branding information displays trust indicators on end-user phones.

🔒

Encryption

SRTP and DTLS protocols provide end-to-end encryption for all voice and media, ensuring complete privacy.

⚡

Direct Routing

Peer-to-peer connections eliminate transit providers, reducing costs by up to 80% and improving call quality.

🔄

Number Porting

Distributed database with eventual consistency enables real-time number portability across all carriers.

🚑

Real-time 999

GPS location data transmitted instantly with emergency calls for life-saving location accuracy.
