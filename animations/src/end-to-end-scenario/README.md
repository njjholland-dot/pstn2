# End-to-End Scenario

One PSTN2 v1.1 call from Alice (Alpha Telecom, 07700 900123) to Bob (Bravo Networks, 0113 496 0456, ported in from Charlie Comms): discovery, verification, encryption, direct media, branding, emergency location and caching.

Runs on the shared broadcast player (`../../shared/`, see its README). About 6.0 minutes of pre-rendered narration, 15 scenes.

## Scenes

1. **Meet Alice and Bob** (`meet`)
2. **Alice dials** (`dial`)
3. **Who has Bob’s number?** (`discover`)
4. **Is it really Alice?** (`verify`)
5. **Agreeing encryption** (`encrypt`)
6. **A direct media path** (`media`)
7. **Who’s calling, verified** (`brand`)
8. **Bob answers** (`answer`)
9. **HD voice** (`quality`)
10. **If Alice dials 999** (`emergency`)
11. **Next time: straight to Bravo** (`learn`)
12. **Today’s PSTN and PSTN2** (`compare`)
13. **The cost of a call** (`cost`)
14. **It just works** (`experience`)
15. **Everything, working together** (`vision`)

## Files

- `narration.json`: the script (captions and voice)
- `scenes.js`: the animated diagram for each scene
- `deck.css`: deck-specific styles
- `audio/`: generated by `node tools/narrate/build-narration.mjs end-to-end-scenario`

Preview: `index.html?capture=1` with `window.__p2.show(i)` for QA; `#n` starts at scene n.
